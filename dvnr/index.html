Devenir
Revolution of the mind

DEVENIR is a groundbreaking interactive installation that pushes the boundaries of human-machine interaction, using the innovative technology of Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCIs). This project allows users to control and build a digital world purely through their thoughts, reimagining how people interact with machines and redefining digital experiences. By connecting brainwaves to commands in Unreal Engine, DEVENIR introduces a new, more intuitive way for users to manipulate and shape digital environments, creating a deeper, more immersive experience than ever before.

A once in a lifetime digital experience

The core technology of DEVENIR is a BCI that translates brain activity into real-time commands, letting users control the environment with their minds. This is achieved through a combination of EEG (electroencephalography) tracking headsets and pattern recognition software, which identifies brainwave patterns and converts them into on-screen actions. The system was developed using Unreal Engine and Unreal Chaos Destruction, ensuring that each interaction is dynamic and unique​. This innovative process allowed us to create a project that feels like a natural extension of the user's mind, redefining what digital interaction can be.
