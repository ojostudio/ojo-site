About us.

At OJO, we are a multidisciplinary team with a global reach, dedicated to creating experiences people will never forget. Combining creativity and technology, we focus on crafting immersive, transformative moments that resonate deeply with audiences. For us, technology is not the focus—it’s the tool we use to deliver unforgettable experiences.

Led by Creative Director Fred Breslau, whose career spans over two decades, our team draws on expertise in design, branding, motion, and interactive media to deliver impactful results. Our work has been recognized with prestigious awards like the Red Dot Award and the Webby, celebrating achievements such as redefining BMW’s identity and groundbreaking projects in human-computer immersion. These accolades showcase our ability to merge artistry and innovation to create extraordinary experiences.

Specializing in VR, AR, AI-powered solutions, and interactive design, we approach each project with the goal of elevating how brands connect with their audiences. Whether it’s a product launch, an interactive installation, or a reimagined brand identity, every detail is crafted to deliver a bigger-than-life experience.

At OJO, we’re not just keeping pace with the future—we’re shaping it. Your vision inspires us to push boundaries, redefine possibilities, and create the kind of experiences that are remembered forever. Let’s make it unforgettable.
